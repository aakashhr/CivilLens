# CivilLens Community Guidelines
These rules are stored in the vector index and retrieved (RAG) so that every verdict cites the rule it is based on.

## G1 No personal attacks
Comments must not insult, name-call or demean any person, official or group. Words such as idiot, fool, clown, useless or brainless aimed at people are personal attacks and must be removed in a rewrite.

## G2 No profanity
Comments must not contain swear words or crude language such as damn, hell, crap or bloody. A rewrite must express frustration without profanity.

## G3 No threats or intimidation
Comments must not threaten, intimidate or warn of harm or consequences to a person, such as "you will regret this" or "we will make sure you lose your job". Any rewrite that keeps a threat is rejected.

## G4 No harassment or dismissal
Comments must not tell people to shut up, get lost, go away or disappear, or say that nobody cares about them. Dismissive and harassing language must be removed.

## G5 No sarcasm or mockery
Mocking praise such as "great job as always", "thanks for nothing" or "slow clap" is hidden toxicity. A rewrite must state the complaint directly instead of mocking.

## G6 Keep the original complaint
A rewrite must keep the citizen's genuine complaint, request or opinion. Removing the issue, the time period or the responsible authority makes the rewrite over-cleaned and unacceptable.

## G7 Do not change the writer's stance
A rewrite must not change a complaint into praise or praise into a complaint. If the writer was against something, the rewrite must remain against it.

## G8 Do not add new facts or accusations
A rewrite must not add claims, numbers, names or accusations such as theft, bribery or corruption that were not in the original comment.

## G9 Respect identity groups
Mentions of religion, gender, sexuality, disability, age or community are not toxic by themselves. They must not be flagged or removed when used respectfully, and must never be attacked.

## G10 Keep it clear and readable
A rewrite must be a complete, readable sentence in plain language, without broken grammar, placeholder tags or repeated punctuation.

## G11 Protect reviewers
Reports and dashboards must never display raw abusive words. Toxic words are shown only as masked category tags such as [INSULT].
